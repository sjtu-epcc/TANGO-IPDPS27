\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we propose \tango{} to improve multi-SLO LLM serving through joint temporal and spatial scheduling.
Its dispatcher uses phase-aware deadlines to guide request admission and preemption according to heterogeneous TTFT and TPOT requirements.
Its allocator uses predicted step time and remaining decode slack to allocate prefill tokens, limiting mixed prefill/decode interference.
The evaluation results show that \tango{} improves overall SLO attainment by 58.72\% and achieves $1.52\times$ the goodput on average compared with state-of-the-art systems.
