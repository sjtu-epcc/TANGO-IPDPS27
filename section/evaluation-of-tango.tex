\section{Evaluation of TANGO}
\label{sec:evaluation-of-tango}

% 总领段提纲：仿照 ELORA 用两句交代评估路线；先比较不同模型与 Multi-SLO 工作负载下的总体性能，再验证两个调度模块、步耗时模型及调度开销。
In this section, we first evaluate the SLO attainment and goodput of \tango{} across different models and multi-SLO workloads.
We then investigate the effectiveness of its two scheduling components, validate the step-time model, and analyze scheduling overhead.

\subsection{Evaluation Setup}
\label{subsec:evaluation-setup}

% Setup 第1段提纲：逐句沿用 ELORA 的组织顺序：平台表、工作负载所在章节、模型及简称、按参数规模部署所需 GPU 数、单卡显存，最后用四类 SLO 对应 ELORA 的 LoRA 数量变化；未确认的 GPU 数量标红。
Table~\ref{tab:motivation-setup} shows our experimental platform.
The chatbot, mathematical-reasoning, and software-agent workloads are described in Section~\ref{subsec:motivation-trace}.
We use Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-14B, and Qwen3-32B (8B, 14B, and 32B for short) as base models.
Based on the parameter size, we use 1, 2, and 4 NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada GPUs to deploy them, respectively.
Each RTX 6000 Ada has 48\,GB of GPU memory.
Moreover, we use four SLO classes (TTFT-prioritized, TPOT-prioritized, balanced, and relaxed) for each model.

% Setup 第2段提纲：仿照 ELORA 在平台与模型段后直接介绍基线；先列出 vLLM、SOLA 和 JITServe 并说明其调度机制，再交代 SOLA 源码未公开、JITServe 版本不同，因此在统一的 vLLM 0.17.0 上实现或迁移其调度机制；未确认的参数配置标红。
We use the LLM serving systems vLLM, SOLA, and JITServe as baselines.
vLLM employs FCFS admission and a fixed maximum per-step token budget, without differentiating request-specific TTFT and TPOT objectives.
SOLA adjusts both request execution order and iteration workload according to request and system states to improve SLO attainment.
JITServe estimates uncertain request characteristics and allocates serving bandwidth to individual requests, while grouping compatible requests to improve batching efficiency.
Since SOLA's source code is not publicly available and JITServe uses a different vLLM version, we reimplement SOLA's scheduling mechanisms and port those of JITServe to vLLM 0.17.0.
This allows all systems to be compared on the same vLLM runtime.

% Setup 第3段提纲：仿照 ELORA 最后一段先列出评估指标，再分别给出定义；整体 SLO 达标率统计同时满足各自 TTFT 和 TPOT 目标的请求比例，goodput 统计每秒完成的此类请求数。
Following prior works, we use overall SLO attainment and goodput as performance metrics.
Overall SLO attainment is the percentage of all requests that satisfy both their TTFT and TPOT SLOs.
Goodput measures the number of such requests completed per second.

\subsection{SLO Attainment and Goodput}
\label{subsec:slo-attainment-and-goodput}

% 总体实验图：用户指定为单栏；图注仿照 ELORA，依次交代两个评估指标、TANGO 与三个基线，以及不同实验场景。
\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/evaluation/slo-attainment-and-goodput.pdf}
  \caption{The overall SLO attainment and goodput of \tango{}, vLLM, SOLA, and JITServe in various scenarios.}
  \label{fig:slo-attainment-and-goodput}
\end{figure}

% Evaluation B 第1段提纲：仿照 ELORA 先说明总体实验的指标与范围；覆盖三种工作负载和三种模型，各组合均采用对应 vLLM 的 1.2 倍 90\% 达标率发送率，所有系统使用相同请求、到达时间及 SLO，最后引出总体实验图。
We first evaluate \tango{} on overall SLO attainment and goodput.
Each workload is evaluated on the 8B, 14B, and 32B models.
For each model--workload combination, we replay the trace at $1.2\lambda_{90}$, where $\lambda_{90}$ is the sending rate at which vLLM achieves 90\% SLO attainment for that combination.
All systems use the same request sequence, arrival schedule, and SLO targets.
Fig.~\ref{fig:slo-attainment-and-goodput} shows the results for all nine model--workload combinations.

% Evaluation B 第2段提纲：仿照 ELORA 将达标率与 goodput 同时领先的结论融入总体结果；先给出九组实验的平均提升，再突出综合提升最大的 Qwen3-32B math 场景，汇报其绝对指标、相对三个基线的提升及跨基线平均值。达标率绝对增量用百分点，goodput 用倍数，不将 goodput 结论扩大为原始吞吐量结论。
As observed, \tango{} achieves the highest overall SLO attainment and goodput in all test cases.
Across the nine cases, the average increases in SLO attainment are 23.37\%, 21.52\%, and 15.48\% compared with vLLM, SOLA, and JITServe, respectively.
The corresponding average goodput ratios are $1.52\times$, $1.47\times$, and $1.22\times$.
The most pronounced combined improvement occurs in the Qwen3-32B mathematical-reasoning case, where \tango{} increases overall SLO attainment  by 42.40\%, 41.50\%, and 18.90\% over vLLM, SOLA, and JITServe, respectively, while the corresponding goodput ratios are $2.50\times$, $2.44\times$, and $1.20\times$.

% Evaluation B 第3段提纲：对应 ELORA 的性能原因解释，联系本文已识别的排队和混合步干扰两类问题；说明 deadline 准入与条件抢占如何服务紧迫请求，预测步耗时如何控制 prefill 对 decode 的干扰；总体图只提供结果，模块贡献留给后续消融验证。
These improvements are consistent with \tango{}'s joint control of request urgency and step duration.
The dispatcher prioritizes earlier deadlines for admission and preempts a later-deadline running request when KV-cache allocation fails.
The allocator uses predicted step time and active latency slack to bound the prefill work sharing a step with decode requests.
This coordination addresses the TTFT violations from queueing and TPOT violations from mixed prefill/decode execution identified in Section~\ref{subsec:motivation-underlying-reasons}.

\subsection{Effectiveness of Temporal Scheduling}
\label{subsec:effectiveness-temporal-scheduling}

% 消融实验图：单栏；图注仿照 ELORA，依次交代消融版本的 goodput 与整体 SLO 达标率、相对完整 TANGO 的归一化，以及柱形和曲线分别对应两个指标。
\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/evaluation/ablation_goodput_slo.pdf}
  \caption{The goodput and overall SLO attainment of \tango{}'s variants normalized to \tango{}, represented by bars and curves, respectively.}
  \label{fig:ablation-goodput-slo}
\end{figure}

% Evaluation C 第1段提纲：仿照 ELORA 先定义 temporal 消融版本 TANGO-WOT；关闭 dispatcher，恢复 vLLM 默认 FCFS 准入及默认抢占，保留 spatial allocator，其他实验条件沿用总体实验。
In this subsection, we show the performance of \tango{}-WOT, a variant of \tango{} that disables temporal scheduling.
It restores vLLM's default admission and preemption policies, including FCFS request admission, while retaining the \allocator{}.
All other experimental conditions, including the nine model--workload combinations and sending rates, are kept the same as in Section~\ref{subsec:slo-attainment-and-goodput}.

% Evaluation C 第2段提纲：仿照 ELORA 指出图中对应的绿色柱形与曲线；给出九组实验的平均相对降幅，再用 goodput 降幅最大的 8B math 场景说明 temporal 的作用。数据从提供的 PDF 矢量绘图坐标恢复；平均相对降幅为 100*(1-mean(variant/TANGO))，不是达标率的百分点差。
The green bars and curves of Fig.~\ref{fig:ablation-goodput-slo} show the goodput and overall SLO attainment of \tango{}-WOT normalized to \tango{}.
As observed, both metrics decrease in all nine cases, with average relative reductions of 20.31\% and 20.39\%, respectively.

% Evaluation C 第3段提纲：解释 temporal 消融后紧迫请求无法获得更早准入，默认抢占也不比较活跃 deadline；spatial 能控制已选 batch 的步耗时，却无法修正等待队列的服务顺序，因此单独保留 spatial 不能恢复完整性能。
Without phase-aware prioritization, FCFS can leave requests with tight TTFT targets waiting behind earlier requests with more latency slack.
The default preemption policy also does not compare active deadlines when deciding which running request should yield KV-cache capacity.
Although the retained allocator regulates the execution time of mixed batches, it cannot by itself give earlier admission to urgent waiting requests.
Consequently, controlling step duration alone is insufficient to recover the SLO attainment and goodput of the complete system.

\subsection{Effectiveness of Spatial Scheduling}
\label{subsec:effectiveness-spatial-scheduling}

% Evaluation D 第1段提纲：仿照 ELORA 定义 spatial 消融版本 TANGO-WOS；关闭模型引导的 token allocator，改用固定的每步最大 token 预算，保留 temporal 的 deadline 准入及条件抢占，实验条件与前一节一致。
In this subsection, we show the performance of \tango{}-WOS, a variant of \tango{} that disables spatial scheduling.
It replaces model-guided token allocation with a fixed maximum token budget per step, while retaining the \dispatcher{}.
Thus, request admission and preemption still use phase-aware deadlines, and all other experimental conditions remain unchanged.

% Evaluation D 第2段提纲：对应图中的蓝色柱形和曲线，汇报九组实验中 goodput 与整体达标率的平均相对降幅；补充 32B math 的具体归一化结果。平均值由图中九组比例计算，采用相对百分比，不使用百分点。
The blue bars and curves of Fig.~\ref{fig:ablation-goodput-slo} show the goodput and overall SLO attainment of \tango{}-WOS normalized to \tango{}.
Both metrics remain below those of \tango{} in every case, with average relative reductions of 13.19\% in goodput and 12.66\% in SLO attainment.


% Evaluation D 第3段提纲：解释固定 token 上限不能约束不同组成及上下文长度下的混合步耗时，长 prefill 会推迟 decode 输出；temporal 负责选择请求，spatial 负责按预测时间决定同一步可执行的 prefill 工作量，两者共同提高达标率及 goodput。
A fixed token budget does not provide a reliable bound on the duration of a mixed prefill/decode step.
As batch composition and context lengths change, the same token count can incur different computation and KV-cache access costs.
A long prefill chunk can therefore delay the next tokens of decode requests even when request admission follows deadline order.
The model-guided allocator controls this interference by adjusting the prefill allocation to the active decode workload and remaining latency slack.
These results show that deadline-aware request ordering and execution-time-aware token allocation make complementary contributions to serving performance.
